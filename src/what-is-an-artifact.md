# What is an "Artifact"?

Artefacts are primarily intended to be electronic hardware of some variety, but could also include brackets and fixings and other associated hardware elements.

Anything that is a distinct piece of hardware would be considered for inclusion. A non-exhaustive list of these is included here below:

- Printed Circuit Boards
- Enclosed electronic devices
- Specific enclosures or mounting hardware
    - Brackets
    - Enclosures or boxes
    - Specialist fixings
- Cables and cable assemblies

Examples of items not acceptable for hwidx.org identifiers are shown in the non-exhaustive list below:

- Groups of other unassociated hardware (bags of screws, nuts, standoffs, etc.)
    - Multiples of items in the approved examples would instead have the same hwidx.org identifier attached individually
- Software or other digital-only items

## Exceptions

Exceptions to the above will be considered on their own merit, and should be discussed in an appropriate Github pull request or comment.

These lists may be revised at a later date. (Last revision October 2025)
